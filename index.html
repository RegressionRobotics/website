About Our Team

Engineering Excellence

Regression Robotics is a competitive FTC team dedicated to pushing the boundaries of robotics innovation. Our team combines cutting-edge engineering with creative problem-solving to build robots that excel in competition.

Founded with a passion for STEM education and competitive excellence, we've grown into a formidable force in the robotics community, consistently ranking among the top teams in our region.
